Overview

About this course.

Can you explain why you are Muslim? Is Islam the true religion? How can we know there is One God? This course explores these fundamental questions through a structured, evidence-based approach that equips students to understand and articulate the rational foundations of Islamic belief.

Developed and taught by Shaykh Hamza Karamali at Basira Education, the course examines the key arguments for the existence and oneness of God while addressing contemporary challenges raised by atheism and other worldviews. It will be taught by Sabeen Sheikh, a certified instructor.

What you will study

Objectives of
this course.

  1. Foundations

    Examine the meaning and foundations of Islam.

  2. Evidence and reason

    Understand how Islamic belief is grounded in evidence and reason rather than personal preference.

  3. Arguments for God

    Study the major rational arguments for the existence and attributes of God, including the Argument from Contingency, the Design Argument and the Argument for the Oneness of God.

  4. Contemporary questions

    Explore questions relating to evolution, evil and suffering through the lens of Islamic rational theology.

  5. Confident conversation

    Develop confidence in answering common questions about faith, belief and the existence of God.
