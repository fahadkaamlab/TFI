Welcome

An institute for the
next generation.

We are an educational and service institute designed to bring the authentic knowledge of Islam to young Muslim adults and its application in the real world. Based in Harrow, we are at the inception stage — building carefully, with small cohorts and serious attention to what young people actually need.

Our teaching is rooted in the Qur’an and Sunnah, carried by scholarship, and shaped for the questions young Muslims are asking today.

Our values

What we draw
our inspiration from.

We draw our inspiration from the Qur’an and the Sunnah of the Prophet (peace be upon him) — a faith that is believed, lived and shared.

01

The Sabiqun (The Foremost)

“As for the foremost, they will be the foremost.”

Surah Al-Waqi’ah (56:10)

We want young Muslims to be among those who race towards good — not held back by the noise around them, but moving forward with conviction and purpose.

02

Belief and good deeds

“And those who believe and do righteous good deeds, they are dwellers of Paradise, they will dwell therein forever.”

Surah Al-Baqarah (2:82)

The Qur’an frequently pairs belief with action. Faith must be accompanied by righteous deeds — a state loved and praised by Allah, with the promise of Paradise in the Hereafter.

03

Spreading good and forbidding evil

“Let there arise out of you a group of people inviting to all that is good, enjoining Al-Ma’ruf and forbidding Al-Munkar. And it is they who are successful.”

Surah Ale-Imraan (3:104)

Building a just and righteous society calls for active participation — through our words, our actions and the positions of responsibility we take.

04

Changemakers and reformers

“And your Lord would not have destroyed the cities unjustly while their people were reformers.”

Surah Hud (11:117)

Allah uses the word Muslihoon (reformers), not merely Saalihoon (the righteous). The righteous may do good for themselves; the reformers create change that ripples out to the community and beyond.

05

Be a palm tree

“See you not how Allah sets forth a parable? A goodly word is as a goodly tree, whose root is firmly fixed, and its branches reach to the sky.”

Surah Ibrahim (14:24)

The Prophet (peace be upon him) likens the believer to a palm tree: deeply rooted, standing tall, resilient in harsh weather, bearing sweet fruit and spreading shade to all around it. We hope the young people we nurture grow in the same way — strong in character, rooted in belief, and beneficial to family, community and society.

Our teacher

Taught with scholarship.
Shared with care.

“After more than 15 years of Islamic learning, Sabeen set up the Institute to bring her knowledge of the Islamic sciences to young Muslim students.”

Sabeen delivered the Institute’s first set of courses in 2025.

Sabeen started her sacred journey of seeking knowledge at Nurul Ilm Academy in Dubai, graduating with an Alimiyya degree on the Darse Nizami curriculum in 2014. She expanded her knowledge at Cambridge Islamic College, Utrujj Foundation, Arabica Institute, the London College of Islamic Studies and Basira Education. A student of Shaykh Haytham Tamim, she volunteers her time at Utrujj transcribing and copy-editing his lectures on Tafseer and the Arbaeen Nawawiyyah.

In 2014, with family and friends, she set up Sohba Saleha in Harrow — an after-school club helping local children build their Islamic identity in a fun, safe environment.

Alongside her husband, Sabeen also ran and delivered Islamic circles for teenage girls in the community for seven years, opening conversations around the challenges young people face today.

In 2023 she began volunteering as a teacher at Turning Point Academy, an education and social centre for revert sisters. In 2025 she received her general ijaza in Hadith, Tafseer and Fiqh from Shaykh Haytham Tamim through the Utrujj Foundation, and teacher certification on the flagship Why Islam is True course from Basira Education. In 2026 she completed her Practitioner Certification in NLP.

An aware humanitarian with a Master’s in Public Health, Sabeen brings a long-standing passion for public health and non-profit work, including global health projects at UNICEF and cancer outreach and education in the US.
