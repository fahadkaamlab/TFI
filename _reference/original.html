Knowledge

Our goal is to deliver the authentic knowledge of Islam to young Muslim adults in an engaging and meaningful way that encourages thinking and reflection and builds a deeper connection with Allah. We hope such knowledge will encourage them to build their own character and empower them to act and lead with goodness in their communities and in the society at large.

Courses

Service​

We aim to encourage the values of service by creating opportunities for young adults to be part of community based programs to serve the needy and the elderly, and to foster the values of stewardship by engaging with local and global initiatives to protect and preserve the environment.

Leadership

In the long run we hope such thinking and effort coupled with skill building and mentoring around leadership will inspire young adults to be vehicles for good, leaving a positive impact in a changing world.
