Our Approach

How will we achieve this?

Knowledge

Phase 1:

Our Foundation courses include:

Our Journey with the Quran (Age 16+): A reflective study of the 30th Part of the Quran, this course begins by introducing students to the historical, social and cultural context in which the Quran was revealed and how the Quran’s message is delivered within this context. We then move on to study the meanings and tafseer of the Quran by beginning with Surah Al Fatiha and covering in detail the last 10 surahs of the 30th Juz.

Life of the Prophet (peace be upon him) (15+): This is a comprehensive course offering more than a historical narrative of the events surrounding the Prophet’s (peace be upon him) life and his mission. It is an opportunity to delve and reflect into the deeper meanings of some of the significant events in his prophetic life and how they can be applied when addressing some of the important challenges Muslims face in the world today.

Why Islam is True (15+): A flagship course devised by Basira Education, this course is a must do for young adults to help them solidify their own rational understanding of belief in One God and to learn how to address some of the counter arguments from contending points of view.

See our courses section for more detail.

Courses

When divine knowledge enters the hearts, it makes the heart of the believer conscious of its purpose, aware of the choices it makes and cognisant of the responsibility to its Creator.

The action of such a believer, is morally upright in every sphere of their life. Their actions are guided by what pleases Allah most.

We hope that the knowledge we deliver enables them to be more conscious believers.

Service

Phase 1: Our goal is to collaborate and partner with local organizations such as soup kitchens, elderly care programs, programs through the local mosque, organizations supporting refugees, efforts to reduce plastic pollution, etc. to establish a link whereby our students can be of service and allocate their time during the year.
